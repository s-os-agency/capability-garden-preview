Core Awareness

The judgment layer that connects intent, context and action.

Core Awareness sits at the heart of the tree. It is the judgment that decides what matters, in what order, and when a result is good enough. Every Level 1 skill draws on it.

Prioritisation

You'll need
Candidate actions, decision criteria, resource limits
Done when
Alternatives are compared on stated criteria; recommendation and tradeoffs follow from evidence; unsupported weights remain assumptions.

SK-045 · Quality & verificationAvailable

Output validation

You'll need
Expected result; source inputs; output artifact; independent read-back
Done when
Check completeness, correctness, and usability against explicit criteria; reread the saved result and record discrepancies.
Supported by
Acceptance checklist; generate-runbook validator
Next action
Validate one completed deliverable against its requirements and independently reread its saved state.
